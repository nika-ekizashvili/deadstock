12 — TABLE ROW

Desktop row, mobile row

The title links to the Instagram post. Actions are icon buttons with tooltips: ✓ approve and ✕ reject while an item is pending, and a SOLD stamp button on approved items that aren't sold yet. On phones the row becomes a stacked list item with 44 px buttons.
